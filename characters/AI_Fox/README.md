# AI Fox — Approved character

Approved by owner for addition to this standalone mascot library.

## Contents
- 12 expression clips: idle, smile, happy, talk, chat, listening, thinking, processing, idea, scanning, connected, sleeping.
- 128 x 64, one-bit monochrome keyframes in `keyframes.zlib.b64`.
- Reproducible PNG export using `python tools/export_fox.py` (requires Pillow).
- 4 sequential frames per clip, with clip-specific timing stored as `meta.json`.

## Important
These are **distinct key poses** with subtle vertical motion generated for clip playback, not fully hand-drawn motion cycles. Do not render the entire contact sheet as an animation. This is a source-art layer, **not yet a directly installable Momentum .bm pack**; firmware-specific conversion and hardware testing are outstanding.

The original Dolphin and other repositories are unaffected. Further characters or newly proposed animations require owner preview and approval before committing.
